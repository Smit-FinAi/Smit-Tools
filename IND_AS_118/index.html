Authoritative MCA Rules
Ind AS 118 Architectural Rule:
  • Standard: Segregates Operating, Investing and Financing categories strictly.
  • Lending / NBFC: Classifies customer interest income and borrowing costs funding loans in the Operating Category.
  • Investing: Classifies dividend, interest, rental and fair value gains in the Operating Category.
